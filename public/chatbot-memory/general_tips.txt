EYE CARE GENERAL TIPS & PREVENTION
=====================================
This file contains general tips and preventive care guidelines for the AI chatbot.

--- SCREEN TIME & DIGITAL EYE STRAIN ---
The 20-20-20 Rule: Every 20 minutes of screen time, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
Optimal screen distance: 20-26 inches from eyes (arm's length).
Screen position: Slightly below eye level to reduce strain.
Lighting: Avoid glare; screen brightness should match surrounding environment.
Blue light: Use blue light filters or night mode in evenings after 6pm.
Blinking: Consciously blink more often during screen use (we blink 50% less when using screens).
Eye drops: Lubricating drops can help combat dry eyes from screen use.
Environment: Use a humidifier if air is dry; avoid direct airflow on eyes from fans/AC.
Breaks: Even with the 20-20-20 rule, take longer breaks every 2 hours.

--- NUTRITION FOR EYE HEALTH ---
Lutein & Zeaxanthin: Found in leafy greens (spinach, kale, collard greens), eggs. Help prevent AMD and cataracts.
Omega-3 Fatty Acids: Found in fatty fish (salmon, tuna, sardines), flaxseeds, walnuts. Important for retinal function, reduces dry eye risk.
Vitamin C: Found in citrus fruits, strawberries, bell peppers, broccoli. Helps prevent cataracts and AMD.
Vitamin E: Found in nuts (almonds, sunflower seeds), vegetable oils. Antioxidant protecting eye cells.
Beta-Carotene/Vitamin A: Found in carrots, sweet potatoes, butternut squash. Maintains corneal health and night vision.
Zinc: Found in oysters, beef, pumpkin seeds, chickpeas. Helps vitamin A produce protective pigment (melanin) in eyes.
Anthocyanins: Found in blueberries, blackberries, dark cherries. May improve night vision.

--- PROTECTIVE MEASURES ---
Sunglasses: Wear sunglasses blocking 99-100% of UVA and UVB radiation year-round outdoors.
Sunglasses types: Choose wrap-around styles for maximum protection; polarized lenses reduce glare.
Safety eyewear: Wear appropriate eye protection for sports (polycarbonate lenses), home repair, yard work, chemical handling.
Contact lens hygiene: Always wash hands before handling; follow replacement schedule; never sleep in lenses (unless approved); never use water to clean; replace case monthly.
Eye rubbing: Avoid rubbing eyes as it can cause corneal damage and spread infection.
Makeup: Remove eye makeup before bed; replace mascara every 2-3 months; don't share eye makeup.

--- LIFESTYLE FACTORS ---
Smoking: Smoking doubles risk of AMD, increases cataract risk, and damages optic nerve. Quitting reduces risk.
Alcohol: Excessive alcohol consumption linked to cataracts and AMD; moderate consumption at most.
Hydration: Drink 8-10 glasses of water daily to maintain tear production and overall eye moisture.
Sleep: 7-9 hours nightly. Eyes repair themselves during sleep. Sleep deprivation causes eye twitching, dryness, blurred vision.
Exercise: Regular physical activity reduces risk of glaucoma (lowers eye pressure) and diabetic retinopathy.
Blood pressure: High blood pressure damages retinal blood vessels; maintain healthy BP below 120/80.
Diabetes management: Tightly controlled blood sugar is the best way to prevent diabetic eye complications.

--- EYE EXAMINATION SCHEDULE ---
Children (birth-2 years): First exam at 6-12 months
Children (3-5 years): At least once in this period
School-age children: Before 1st grade, then annually
Adults (18-39): Every 2 years if no risk factors; annually if contacts worn or risk factors present
Adults (40-54): Every 1-2 years; increased AMD/glaucoma risk screening
Adults (55-64): Every 1-2 years
Adults (65+): Annually
High-risk individuals: Annually regardless of age (diabetes, family history of glaucoma/AMD, high myopia, previous eye surgery)

--- CONTACT LENS CARE ---
Handwashing: Always wash and dry hands before touching lenses.
Cleaning: Use only recommended lens solution; never use water, saliva, or homemade saline.
Sleeping: Never sleep in contacts unless specifically approved by eye doctor (ortho-k lenses).
Swimming: Remove contacts before swimming or wear waterproof goggles.
Replacement: Follow prescribed replacement schedule (daily, bi-weekly, monthly).
Case care: Rinse case with solution (not water), air dry face-down, replace case every 1-3 months.
Warning signs: Remove lenses immediately if eye becomes red, irritated, painful, or vision becomes blurry.

--- FIRST AID FOR EYE EMERGENCIES ---
Chemical splash: Immediately flush with clean water for 15-20 minutes; call poison control; seek emergency care.
Foreign object: Don't rub; try blinking; rinse with clean water or saline; if embedded, cover and go to ER.
Eye injury/trauma: Cover eye gently; don't apply pressure; go to emergency room immediately.
Sudden vision loss: Medical emergency - call emergency services or go to ER immediately.
Severe eye pain: Seek immediate medical attention.
Flashes + floaters + curtain over vision: Possible retinal detachment - EMERGENCY care needed within hours.

--- COMPUTER VISION SYNDROME (CVS) TIPS ---
Monitor settings: Adjust text size, contrast, and color temperature.
Ergonomics: Position screen 15-20 degrees below eye level; get an anti-glare screen.
Ambient lighting: Avoid glare from windows; position monitor perpendicular to windows.
Prescription glasses: Consider computer/anti-reflective glasses if using screens 6+ hours daily.
Artificial tears: Use preservative-free lubricating drops 4x daily if needed during screen work.
