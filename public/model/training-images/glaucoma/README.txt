Note: Glaucoma is best detected via fundus/retinal photography. External eye images may not show clear signs.
For external images, look for:
- Enlarged optic cup (cup-to-disc ratio > 0.6 in fundus images)
- Optic nerve pallor (pale optic disc)
- Neuroretinal rim thinning

For best results, use fundus photography (retinal images) for glaucoma detection.
Sources: ORIGA dataset, RIM-ONE dataset, Drishti dataset.
