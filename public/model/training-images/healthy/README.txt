Image requirements:
- Minimum 100 images (ideally 500+)
- Clear front-facing close-up of the eye
- Well-lit, in-focus

Example healthy eye characteristics to look for:
- Clear white sclera (no redness)
- Clear cornea
- Normal pupil (round, equal size, reacts to light)
- No discharge or swelling
- Normal eyelid position
